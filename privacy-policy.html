Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how M-incognito Resources LLP ("DigiStar", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit our website or contact us through it.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us through our contact form or by email:

  • Full name and company name
  • Work email address
  • Phone or WhatsApp number (optional)
  • The message you write to us

Our servers may also record basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, which we use for security and to keep the site running.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss our services with you
  • To contact you by email, phone or WhatsApp about your request
  • To protect the website from spam and misuse
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing your information

We share your information only with service providers that help us operate the website and handle your enquiry (for example, hosting and email providers), and only as needed for those purposes. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for advertising or tracking. If this changes, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and for any follow-up discussion, and for any longer period the law requires. After that, we delete or anonymise them.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to access, correct or erase your personal data, and you may withdraw your consent at any time. To do so, write to us at the email address below.

8. Third-party links

Our website may link to other sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies before sharing information with them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
